Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who uses it. Adam's Morning Desk is used by one person only, its owner, on his own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it uses. With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's own Google Calendar events and Gmail messages in order to build the owner's daily brief. It does not send email, change calendar events or delete anything.

Where the data goes. The data is processed on the owner's own computer and the brief is saved for the owner's own use. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Removing access. Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account's security settings (Third-party apps with account access).

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
